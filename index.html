memecoin accumulation monitor

How the score works (0–100)

  • Buy pressure (25) — share of buy transactions in the last 1h and 5m, weighted by how active trading is.
  • Volume acceleration (25) — last hour's volume vs its 6h hourly average, and the last 5m vs the hour's pace.
  • Quiet accumulation (20) — buyers dominate and volume rises while price is still roughly flat. The "before the move" pattern.
  • Liquidity health (15) — liquidity at 5–35% of market cap and a solid dollar depth.
  • Live trend (15) — from this page's own snapshots: is liquidity and mcap growing, are buyers consistently in control over the last ~15 min.
  • Penalties — already pumped (+250%/24h, +80%/1h), sellers in control, liquidity being pulled, barely trading, RugCheck danger flags.

Candidates come from DexScreener (new profiles, boosts, community takeovers) and GeckoTerminal (trending, new, most active pools). Only tokens that have a DEX pool are visible — pump.fun bonding-curve tokens appear after they graduate.

The score is a filter for what deserves a manual look, not a prediction. Most memecoins go to zero. Not financial advice.

Data: DexScreener · GeckoTerminal · RugCheck. Runs entirely in your browser; settings and history are kept locally.
