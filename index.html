What SolMon looks for

Only graduated pump.fun / letsbonk coins (left the bonding curve for PumpSwap or Raydium) that have bled out, built a floor, and are starting to wake up.

Score (0–100)

  • Off the top (20) — drawdown from the all-time high. Counts from −50%, full at −70% to −92%, fades to zero past −97% (dead).
  • Floor holding (25) — price within ~35% of its post-ATH floor, the floor is at least ~12h old, it has been retested, and the last 6h isn't bleeding.
  • Sellers exhausted (20) — buys ≥ sells over 6h and 1h, and the 12h price range is tighter than before.
  • Waking up (25) — hourly volume over the last 6h vs the day before, last hour vs the 6h pace, 5m buyers, and an early lift (not a pump yet).
  • Liquidity health (10) — liquidity at 8–50% of MCap and real dollar depth.
  • Penalties — already 2×+ off the low, still making new lows, +50% in 1h, sellers in control, liquidity being pulled, wash-like volume, no socials, RugCheck warnings.

Filters

  • Safe only — RugCheck must have checked the coin and found no danger flags (mint/freeze authority, top-holder concentration, single big holder, low liquidity, rugged). Also hides MCap under $30K, barely trading, bot spam, odd liquidity, and −97%+ coins.
  • Near bottom — at least 60% below ATH and no more than 60% above the floor.

ATH and floor come from GeckoTerminal hourly candles (closing prices, last ~40 days). Candles and RugCheck are pulled a few coins at a time, so the list fills in over the first few minutes.

"Safe" only means the known rug signals are absent. A bottomed memecoin can still go to zero, and devs or whales can still dump. The score is a filter for what deserves a manual look, not a prediction. Not financial advice.
